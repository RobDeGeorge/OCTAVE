Jeep TJ Glove Box Mount - Rev4 OPI5 Magnetic

Source geometry:
- Jeep TJ Glove Box Mount Rev3.STL
- Orange Pi fit and magnet pattern recovered from opi5plus_case_with_magnets.STL

What changed:
- Added a 4.25 mm flat backplate on the stepped/counterbored side of Rev3.
- Added two side guides and a bottom ledge so the OPI5 case slides down into a
  repeatable position before the magnets pull it flat.
- Added all 18 matching magnet pockets in the case's exact asymmetric layout.
- Preserved access to all four Jeep fasteners with 12.4 mm access openings.
- Raised the Orange Pi case 15 mm above the bottom of the Jeep bracket so the
  magnet pockets do not collide with the lower fastener access openings.

Magnets:
- The source OPI5 case has 18 nominal 5.5 mm diameter embedded cavities.
- Rev4 uses 5.6 mm diameter x 3.1 mm deep face-open pockets, suitable for
  nominal 5 x 3 mm round magnets with a small FDM/glue allowance.
- Test every magnet's polarity against the finished OPI5 case before gluing.
- Install magnets flush or slightly below the cradle face.

Fit/orientation:
- The OPI5 case's flat bottom faces the new backplate.
- Its native Y=0 edge is at the top of the cradle.
- Slide the case downward between the side guides until it rests on the ledge.
- Side clearance is 0.45 mm per side and is parametric in the SCAD source.

Printing:
- Print and fit-test before vehicle installation.
- The model is designed as one solid; choose orientation/supports appropriate
  for your printer and material.
- Existing Rev3 files are preserved.
